First, two big words

What is a District Assembly?

Imagine your neighbourhood as a huge team — the District Assembly is like the coaches and leaders of that team!

They are the local government people whose job is to take care of the town: fix roads, build schools, keep streets clean, and make sure everyone stays safe and happy.

Big word

Local government — The leaders in charge of your own district, rather than the whole country.

3

And the second one

What is a District Plan?

A district plan is like a giant map made by local leaders to make a town or city a much better place to live!

Instead of adults just guessing what to do, the plan lists the biggest problems — dirty streets, schools that need help, places that need safer parks — then writes a step-by-step checklist for fixing them.

The Builsa North Municipal Assembly in the Upper East Region made its very own district plan — for every boy and girl, no matter where they live or what special abilities they have!

Big words

Builsa North Municipal Assembly — The local government team in charge of Builsa North, with its offices in Sandema.

Upper East Region — Ghana is split into sixteen big areas called regions. The Upper East Region is in the far north-east of Ghana.

A Brand-New Nursery for Little Ones

Creche in public schools

A safe, fun first classroom for our very youngest — ages 0 to 3!

Building a creche

A safe, fun space right inside public schools, where our youngest children (ages 0–3) can learn and play!

Super teachers

Training local teachers on the very best ways to care for and teach little toddlers.

Big words

Creche — A nursery where babies and toddlers are looked after, play and learn.

Toddlers — Little children who have just learned to walk — usually between one and three years old.

Safe Spaces to Play and Grow

Safe environment

Fun, safe places to explore — and a check-up so every child gets the help they need!

5 zone learning centres

A fun, safe centre in every zone, where children can play, explore and learn.

Health check-ups

Checking children every year, starting early, so anyone who needs extra help or disability support gets it right away!

Big words

Zone — Builsa North is split into smaller areas called zones, so there is a centre in every part of the district.

Disability support — Extra help, tools or care for children whose bodies or brains work in their own way.

Awesome Classrooms for Everyone

Teaching & learning tools

Every child can roll, walk or run right into class!

Easy access for all

Fixing up 10 kindergarten classrooms with ramps and smooth paths, so children using wheelchairs or leg braces can join in easily.

Fun toys & books

Filling classrooms with exciting learning toys and books that every child can use.

Big words

Kindergarten — The first classes at school, for children aged about four and five.

Ramps — A gentle slope instead of steps, so a wheelchair, a pram or wobbly legs can roll or walk right in.

Strong Bodies & Fresh Food

Better nutrition

Fresh food grown with sunshine — even when it doesn't rain!

Sun-powered farms

10 solar-powered gardens across our communities, so families can grow fresh, healthy fruit and vegetables all year long — even when it doesn't rain!

Tasty cooking shows

Showing families how to cook yummy, healthy meals — and sharing fresh food with school lunch programmes.

Big words

Nutrition — The good stuff inside food that helps your body and your brain grow big and strong.

Solar-powered — Using sunshine to make electricity. In a garden it can run a pump that brings water to the plants.

Finding every child

Checking in with communities to make sure no child gets left behind when it's time to start school.

No child left behind

Why six super powers and not one

You can't build a better Builsa North with one block

Imagine building Builsa North out of giant toy blocks. Every colour is one of the six super powers — take a colour away and the whole town wobbles!

1. Happy Families2. New Nursery3. Safe Spaces4. Awesome Classrooms5. Fresh Food6. Ready for School

Fresh food is great — but not if a child misses the first day of school. Ramps help — but not if little ones have nowhere safe to play. When all six super powers work together, every child in Builsa North gets the chance to shine.
