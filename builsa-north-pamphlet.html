Understanding the plan

Our 6 Super Powers

Builsa North's leaders are focusing on six priority areas — every one points back at the same thing: every child in Builsa North.

1

Happy & Helpful Families

Better parenting

Love and great tips make every home a happy home!

  • Trusted helpers — chiefs, community leaders and local heroes share tips on showing children love and support.
  • Friendly chat spots — helpful stories at durbars, churches, mosques, on the radio and at local gatherings.
Community durbarsChurches & mosquesRadio showsLocal gatherings
2

A Brand-New Nursery for Little Ones

Creche in public schools

A safe, fun first classroom for our very youngest — ages 0 to 3!

  • Building a creche — a safe, fun space inside public schools for children aged 0–3.
  • Super teachers — training local teachers to care for and teach little toddlers.
Inside 1
3

Safe Spaces to Play and Grow

Safe environment

Fun, safe places to explore — and a check-up so every child gets the help they need!

  • 5 zone learning centres — a fun, safe centre in every zone to play, explore and learn.
  • Health check-ups — every year, early on, so extra help and disability support come right away.
4

Awesome Classrooms for Everyone

Teaching & learning tools

Every child can roll, walk or run right into class!

  • Easy access for all — ramps and smooth paths for 10 kindergarten classrooms.
  • Fun toys & books — exciting learning toys and books every child can use.
5

Strong Bodies & Fresh Food

Better nutrition

Fresh food grown with sunshine — even when it doesn't rain!

  • Sun-powered farms — 10 solar-powered gardens for fresh fruit and vegetables all year round.
  • Tasty cooking shows — yummy, healthy meals, and fresh food for school lunches.
Inside 2
6

Ready for School on Day One!

Timely enrolment

When the school year starts, every child is in class!

  • Finding every child — checking in with communities so no child gets left behind. No child left behind!
  • Radio & community fun — radio messages and visits so little ones are registered on time.
