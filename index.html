First, two big words

Who makes the plan — and what is it?

Grown-ups use some tricky words for this. Here is what they actually mean.

What is a District Assembly?

Imagine your neighbourhood as a huge team — the District Assembly is like the coaches and leaders of that team!

They are the local government people whose job is to take care of the town: fix roads, build schools, keep streets clean, and make sure everyone stays safe and happy.

What is a District Plan?

A district plan is like a giant map made by local leaders to make a town or city a much better place to live!

Instead of adults just guessing what to do, the plan lists the biggest problems — dirty streets, schools that need help, places that need safer parks — then writes a step-by-step checklist for fixing them.

Why six super powers and not one

You can't build a better Builsa North with one block

Imagine building Builsa North out of giant toy blocks. Every colour is one of the six super powers — take a colour away and the whole town wobbles.

Fresh food is great — but not if a child misses the first day of school. Ramps help — but not if little ones have nowhere safe to play. When all six super powers work together, every child in Builsa North gets the chance to shine.
